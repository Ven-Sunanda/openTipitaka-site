OpenTipitaka™ — Multilingual Pali Tipiṭaka reading, translation, and proofreading platform
Official website: https://www.opentipitaka.org

Read, compare, search, and proofread the Pali Tipitaka—comfortably on any device.

OpenTipitaka is a multilingual Pali Tipiṭaka digitization project jointly undertaken by Maha Vajiralongkorn Pali Theravada Royal College and Pyin Oo Lwin Pa-Auk Tawya Meditation Centre. Under the guidance of Venerable Kumārābhivaṃsa Sayadaw, the project uses modern technology to connect the Pali scriptures with contemporary multilingual study needs and to build a trustworthy platform for translating, parallel reading, and proofreading the Theravāda Tipiṭaka, Aṭṭhakathā, and Ṭīkā. OpenTipitaka provides a cross-platform reading application for Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and Linux. It supports paragraph-aligned reading of the Pali original and multilingual translations, full-text search, professional dictionaries, bookmarks, split-screen reading, automatic scrolling, and text-to-speech, while inviting users worldwide to help proofread and improve the translations. The OpenTipitaka application is designed and developed by Venerable Sunanda, with operational support provided by Dhamma Sarana LLC.

Translation languages currently supported include Chinese, English, Vietnamese, Thai, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Burmese, Sinhala, Marathi, Khmer, Bengali, and Tibetan. More languages will be added over time.

During AI translation, the vast majority of suttas, most commentaries, and some sub-commentaries were translated with reference to Myanmar word-by-word explanations (Nissaya) and Myanmar translations. Overall, the translations are approaching human translation quality and have high reference value. There is still room to improve accuracy, terminology consistency, and related areas; we will continue improving them and update them periodically.

What you can do

A reading workspace designed for deep study: fast navigation, parallel translations, and smooth proofreading.

⟡

Parallel reading

Read Pali original and multiple translations by paragraph. Choose which languages to display.

⌕

Full-text search

Search Pali or translations across the current book or all books, with phrase search support.

✎

Proofreading & editing

Double-click to edit translation paragraphs. Log in to submit proofreading contributions.

☰

Fast navigation

Tipitaka tree + in-book contents. Jump by paragraph or multiple page numbering systems.

𐃌

Dictionary workflow

Tap a Pali word or type one in the dictionary. Optional fuzzy matching helps with long/short vowels, ṭ/t, ḍ/d, ḷ/l, and nasal marks.

▦

Split-screen & tabs

Open multiple books in tabs and show them side-by-side on wide screens. Your layout is remembered.

▸

Auto-scroll & read-aloud

Auto-scroll for long reading sessions and optional translation read-aloud with media controls.

⟠

Pali scripts

Display Pali in multiple scripts (Roman, Devanagari, Thai, Myanmar, Tibetan, and more).

FAQ

Are translations fully proofread?

Not yet. Many translations are AI-generated and still improving. The app invites everyone to help proofread and refine terminology consistency over time.

Can I contribute corrections?

Yes. Register and log in, then double-click a paragraph translation to edit and submit it for review.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Content is stored in local SQLite databases. You can import databases and update them when online.

What if database download fails in the app?

Use the SqlLite Database link in the Download section to download a package, then import it into the app. On Android without Google Play, use the Tipitaka folder link for the APK.
